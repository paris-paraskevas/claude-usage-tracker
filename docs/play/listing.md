# Play Store listing copy

**App name** (≤30): `Usage Tracker for Claude`

**Short description** (≤80):
`Your Claude Code 5h & weekly limits on your phone — widget, alerts, team pool.`

**Full description** (≤4000):
```
Usage Tracker for Claude shows your Claude Code usage on your phone — the same 5-hour and
weekly limits the /usage command shows — plus burn rate, context window, sessions and
all-time stats, with a home-screen widget and optional alerts when you're near a limit.

It pairs with the free desktop app: open Settings → Remote (phone) and scan the QR.
Your phone then mirrors your live usage from anywhere.

FEATURES
• 5-hour and weekly usage with live reset countdowns
• Burn rate: how fast you're using the window and when you'd hit 100%
• Per-model weekly limits (Opus / Sonnet) when your plan has them
• Extra-usage spend this month, when overage is enabled
• A one-glance verdict (All clear / Ease up / Near limit)
• Home-screen widget (resizable) and an optional lock-screen usage notification
• Several desktops/accounts on one phone — switch with a tap
• Team pool: if your desktop is signed in to a team, see every pooled account's 5h / weekly
  load, monthly spend and who's near a limit
• Per-session context-window fill, recent-usage chart and all-time totals
• Claude service status (Ok / Errors / Down)
• Optional push notifications for usage thresholds, burn rate and context alerts
• Light and dark theme, TalkBack-friendly

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• End-to-end encrypted: the relay only ever stores ciphertext — nobody but your own
  devices can read your data.
• Your Claude login never leaves your computer. The phone only reads; it can't run anything
  on your desktop.
• No ads, no analytics, no trackers.

REQUIREMENTS
• The free Claude Usage Tracker desktop app (the source of the data).
• Works while your desktop is running and online.

Open source (MIT): github.com/paris-paraskevas/claude-usage-tracker

DISCLAIMER

```
